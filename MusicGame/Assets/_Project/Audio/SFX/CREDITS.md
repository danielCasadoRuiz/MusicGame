# Sound effects and vocalisations — origin and licence

This file lists every sound effect and vocalisation shipped with the game, by folder. Each cue (`Audio/Sfx/Cues/**/*.asset`) also records its source in its `credits` field.

## `Kenney/` — Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), **CC0 1.0** (public domain)
Commercial use is allowed and no attribution is required (credit appreciated: "Kenney, www.kenney.nl"). Each subfolder keeps the original `License.txt`.

| Folder | Kenney pack | Files used | Game events |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Impact` | Impact Sounds | impactPunch_medium_000–004, impactPunch_heavy_000–004, impactPlate_light_000–003 | light / heavy hit, blocked hit |
| `Digital` | Digital Audio | phaserUp4, phaserUp6, powerUp7 | SPECIAL launch |
| `SciFi` | Sci-fi Sounds | lowFrequency_explosion_000–001, forceField_000–002, laserLarge_000–002, impactMetal_000–002 | KO fall impact, chaser wake / activation / contact |
| `Interface` | Interface Sounds | error_004, error_006, error_008 | trapped by an obstacle |

## `Vocal/HaelDB/` — HaelDB, "Male Grunt/Yelling sounds", **CC0 1.0**
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds (see `LICENSE.txt` in that folder).
- **What we use:** 44 recorded takes without words, trimmed and peak-normalised. They are the characters' hurt, effort and final-defeat vocalisations.
- **Voices:** one vocalist per character group, each composer with its own slight pitch. The player uses a vocalist no composer uses.
