# Song card textures
Generated with the built-in image generation tool, one image per music style, using that style's existing selected button as the reference. Original output 2048 × 768; Unity imports compressed at a maximum width of 512. The images are shared by songs of the same style.

Final prompt brief: recompose the supplied style button as a true 8:3 rectangular song card, retaining its own palette, material, fine frame and distinctive motifs. Keep the left 70% quiet for bold titles, concentrate details on the far right and corners. Straight 90-degree ends; no stretched trapezoids, text, icons, inner boxes, controls, external shadows or padding.

Per-style art direction:
- Acoustic: sage green, parchment scratches, cream/copper rim.
- Catchy: cyan/blue, pink/cyan circles, vivid pink edge.
- Chill: pale icy turquoise, ribbon wave and tiny circles, silver/cyan edge.
- Cinematic: ivory/champagne, gold frame, fine metallic scratches. Targeted correction removed accidental lettering.
- Dance: pearl lavender, magenta/violet facets and luminous rim.
- Dark: deep violet/charcoal, purple strokes and fine mesh.
- Dreamy: periwinkle/violet, orbital arcs and luminous pearls.
- Electronic: saturated blue, cyan/silver edge, geometric filaments.
- Energetic: acid yellow, black/white corner strokes.
- Experimental: lime, black halftone and angled bars.
- Funky: cream, orange halftone dots, purple curved lines.
- Groove: golden yellow, amber rim, purple corner accents.
- Heavy: blood red/charcoal scratched steel and crimson rim.
- Jazzy: navy, gold Art Deco geometry.
- Rock: red/black scratched metal, silver scuff and red edge.
- Soulful: deep petrol teal silk waves, flowing gold ribbons.
