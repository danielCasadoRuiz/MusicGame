# Scattered sheet music

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Transparent RGBA source copied to `MusicGame/Assets/_Project/Art/UI/Jukebox/ScatteredScores.png` (2172 × 724). The existing cabinet and accepted jukebox screenshot were supplied as style/placement references. The cabinet artwork itself was not regenerated.

## Final prompt

Create ONE production-ready TRANSPARENT PNG foreground sprite for this existing game UI. References are STYLE and placement context ONLY. Output ONLY scattered aged sheet-music papers, NOT the cabinet, NOT UI, NOT background, NOT a ground plane. Ultra-wide composition about 4:1, warm ivory parchment with sepia-black musical staves/notes, delicately worn edges, curled corners, painterly polished semi-realistic videogame prop art matching the reference cabinet's music sheets. A playful obsessive composer's chaotic paper spill: 9 to 12 distinct loose music pages, readable front-facing surfaces, some angled 15–40 degrees, a few lightly folded/floating, layered with soft LOCAL contact shadows between pages. Much less foreshortening than papers lying on a realistic floor. Two asymmetrical clusters: substantial left cluster occupying left 0–34% of width, a smaller right cluster occupying 73–100%; one small detached airborne page near far left and one near far right. Middle 38–68% of canvas MUST remain completely transparent from top to bottom to leave the game's Back button unobstructed. The clusters have irregular silhouettes, no straight horizontal baseline, sheets gently fan out and tumble sideways. Overall art should fit a shallow horizontal lower-edge overlay, widest dimension dominant, no vertical tower. Nothing touches/crops at canvas edges, leave 3% transparent padding. No floor, no table, no furniture, no cast shadow onto an imaginary plane, no glow, no text labels, no logos, no buttons. Real alpha transparency including the large central negative space. These sprites will overlap ONLY the lower wooden plinth of the cabinet, telling a story of a composer obsessed with music.

## Integration

Four non-interactive RawImages select regions from the same texture: two main clusters and two individual sheets. Their aspect ratios match the source regions. Original cabinet art remains in place; a UI mesh effect omits its old floor strip. Papers have a separate root and do not receive the existing 22-unit control adjustment. The cabinet and its controls share 90/50-unit framing insets at 1080 high.
